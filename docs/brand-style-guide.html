01 · Name & wordmark

Three candidates, one recommendation

Each name was checked against the App Store and search in November 2026. None is a well-known app in this category. Verso, By Heart, Abide and Lectio were considered and dropped as taken.

02 · Typography

Newsreader for the word, Instrument Sans for the tools

Newsreader carries scripture, titles and the wordmark; its optical-size axis makes 19px verse text sturdy on a phone while the 48px splash stays fine. Instrument Sans does the quiet work: buttons, inputs, labels, numbers that line up.

Newsreader

Production Type. Variable, opsz 6–72, true italics. Designed for long-form on-screen reading. Weights 400 and 500 only; 600 for a single big number.

Instrument Sans

Rodrigo Fuenzalida. Slightly narrow humanist grotesk, tabular figures, enough character to not read as a system default. Weights 400, 500, 600.

Few parts, clearly stated

07 · Voice & tone

Encouraging, unhurried, never cutesy

Second person, present tense, plain words. No exclamation marks outside a quoted verse. No emoji. Scripture is quoted exactly and attributed. Say what a thoughtful person would say out loud.

08 · Iconography

Lucide, 1.5px, nothing filled

One set, one weight. Lucide at 24px with a 1.5px stroke; 1.75px at 16–20px so small icons do not thin out. Round caps and joins. Icons are ink-3 at rest, ink when active, accent only for the active tab and the listening mic. No emoji, no sparkle, no fills. The ribbon is a brand mark, never a tab icon.

09 · Motion

Three durations, one easing, and a short list of what never moves

Durations · easing cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) entering, cubic-bezier(.45,0,.2,1) leaving

120msPress, toggle, hover. A button darkening, a pill selecting.
200msA typed word settling into place, a rung becoming active, a fade.
320msA sheet sliding up, a screen change, the progress fill growing.
1.6sThe one ambient motion: the mic ring breathing (opacity .25 → .7) while listening.

Never animates

  • Scripture text. No typewriter, no word-by-word fade on Today.
  • The tab bar and the wordmark.
  • Streak and score numbers. No count-up.
  • Confetti, particles, sparkles, trophies.
  • The page background on theme change.

prefers-reduced-motion sets every duration to 0 and stops the mic pulse. The app is complete with no motion at all.

10 · Do / Don't

The rules that keep it from looking generated

Do

  • Set scripture in Newsreader at 19px, line-height 1.6, no more than 65 characters a line.
  • Let whitespace separate sections; hairlines only between list rows.
  • Keep one accent and use it for actions and progress only.
  • Use real references and real verse text in every example and empty state.
  • Keep every tap target at 44px or larger.
  • Make the dark theme warm.
  • Write copy a thoughtful person would say out loud.

Don't

  • Don't use Inter, Roboto, system-ui or Space Grotesk as a primary face.
  • Don't use gradients, glass blur, lavender or indigo, or a second accent hue.
  • Don't stack rounded cards with shadows; one bordered verse card per screen at most.
  • Don't use emoji or sparkle icons anywhere.
  • Don't center or justify verse text.
  • Don't animate scripture, numbers, or the tab bar.
  • Don't add badges, trophies or fire. Don't write "Great job!" or "Oops".
  • Don't show a percentage when "3 of 7" says it better.
  • Don't use pure black or pure white as a page ground.
